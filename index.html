Pre-pay ordering platform

ScoopPass lets families order and pay before the fundraiser — so vendors know exact counts and schools skip the cash chaos.

It’s a simple web portal for school nights, PTO dessert sales, and event vendors. You share a link or QR code. Parents order on their phone. On event day you hand out prepaid orders from a list sorted by class or teacher.

In one line

ScoopPass = online pre-orders + payment + pickup lists for fundraiser-style events — not a full POS, not a catalog fundraiser company.

How a ScoopPass event runs

From setup to pickup in four steps

Whether you’re the vendor or the school organizer, the flow is the same: set the offer, share the link, collect prepaid orders, fulfill with a sorted list.

  1. Create the event Menu, prices, date, and cutoff go into the portal.
  2. Share the link Email, Facebook, flyer QR — families order on any phone.
  3. Orders come in paid Vendors see live counts. Schools see participation grow.
  4. Event-day handoff Print class/teacher lists and serve prepaid orders fast.
A real ScoopPass event — 375 orders, 33 teachers, organized in minutes.
