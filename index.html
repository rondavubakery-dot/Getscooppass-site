Prepay in three simple steps.

  1. Create your offer. Set up your event, fundraiser, or menu in minutes—name your items, quantities, prices, and dates.
  2. Share your ScoopPass link. Post your link and QR code on Facebook, email, flyers, and texts. Families and guests buy ahead from any device.
  3. Serve with speed and confidence. On event day, scan QR codes or check names off the list. You know exactly what to bring and who ordered.
