Fundraisers shouldn’t feel like a full-time job.

  • Volunteers are tired of organizing complex catalog campaigns.
  • Counting cash and checks takes forever and is prone to errors.
  • Families are confused about order forms and deadlines.
  • You struggle to see clear totals, payouts, and participation.

Upgrade to a simple digital prepay.

Dessert fundraiser in four steps.

  1. Define your goal. Decide how much you want to raise and what you’re funding.
  2. Choose your dessert partner. Work with your own dessert vendor or we’ll help you connect with one.
  3. Set up your ScoopPass fundraiser page. Pick items, prices, dates, and pickup details.
  4. Share the link. Families order online; you watch the totals grow in real time.

Less volunteer work, more clear results.

  • Online ordering only. No paper forms or cash counting.
  • Real-time tracking. See orders, revenue, and payouts at a glance.
  • Flexible pickup. Serve dessert at a game, event night, or drive-through.
  • Transparent profits. Know exactly how much your group earned.
