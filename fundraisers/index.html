Questions schools ask

Do parents need an app?

No. They open a link or scan a QR code on any phone and check out in the browser.

Can we still sell day-of?

Yes. Pre-pay covers the bulk of orders so you know counts; you can still offer limited day-of sales if you want.

Who handles the food?

Your dessert vendor or approved partner. ScoopPass is the pre-pay and organization layer — not a replacement for a real baker or truck.

How fast can we launch?

If you have a date and a vendor, setup can happen in a single planning call plus a short portal setup — often ready the same week.

We’re in the Waukesha area — can you help us run one?

Yes. Email rondavubakery@gmail.com with your school, date, and rough headcount. We’ll map a simple pilot.
